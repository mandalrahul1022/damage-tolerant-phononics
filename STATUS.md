## Phase 3 tracks

- **Track A — parametric Gmsh unit cell:** scaffold landed. `scripts/mesh_unit_cell.py` produces real 3D OCC geometry at `r = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5` with tet counts in range. Periodic facet pairing is **subset-consistent only** (cKDTree tolerance on group centroids); does **not** use `gmsh.model.mesh.setPeriodic`, so +/- facet triangulations are not guaranteed node-matched. `dolfinx_mpc.MultiPointConstraint` will silently drop or mispair on this mesh. Remediation pending (Subtask 3).
- **Track B — homogeneous-cube Bloch PBC verification:** IMPLEMENTED and validated by real run. Current solver setup: `TARGET_MAGNITUDE` at `sigma=0`, `n_eigs=15`, relative nullspace clamp (`|lambda| < 1e-6 * lambda_max -> 0`), MPC mass assembly with `diagval=0.0` to avoid artificial constrained-row modes, and cached geometry/forms across `solve_at_k` calls.
